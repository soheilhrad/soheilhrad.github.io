From January to May 2026, the internet was cut off. None of us chose it.

During that time, we were testing a script that runs an AI model locally, on a laptop. Everything that relied on the cloud stopped. The local model kept working.

Learning shouldn't stop when the connection does.

[Camino](/projects/camino/) is built for that situation. It's a free Spanish tutor for beginners, with explanations in Persian, English or Spanish, and it runs entirely on the learner's own computer. After setup it needs no internet, and nothing they type leaves the machine.

If you'd like to try it, or use it with people you work with, [ask for it](/projects/camino/).
