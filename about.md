I'm Sohi, and dos (Design of <span lang="fa" dir="rtl">سهی</span>) is where I make small,
creative apps with AI, in English, Spanish and Persian. My work grows out of psychology,
migration work and computational social science, and my research covers human–AI attachment,
digital education and the emotions we hear in music. Each app does one thing, says plainly when
it is an AI, and keeps what you type on your own device wherever it can. For commissions or
collaborations, write to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).
